# LVOVD v2.7.0 — Local Audio Formats & Waveform Editing

## Local audio processing

Local Media now offers standalone Opus, FLAC, and WAV, plus Opus/FLAC audio encoding inside MKV. Audio-only sources can use the existing reversible trim and middle-cut controls. Processing applies committed cuts directly to the selected original stream; temporary playback and waveform assets never become conversion inputs.

Opus uses 48 kHz variable bitrate, initially 64 kbps per channel. The bitrate control remains available within channel-dependent encoder limits. Supported canonical layouts are preserved; `5.1(side)` is refused rather than silently remapped.

FLAC/WAV preserve sample rate and supported channel layout automatically. FLAC accepts proven integer precision through 24 bits, producing 16 or 24 bits without reduction. Floating-point, unknown, and greater-than-24-bit precision cannot be encoded to FLAC here; choose WAV when precision can be inspected. Many compressed AAC/MP3/Opus sources decode to floating-point samples and therefore cannot use this strict FLAC policy.

WAV preserves integer precision through 32 bits or floating-point precision at 32/64 bits, with automatic RF64 for large outputs. Converting lossy audio does not recover discarded detail. Untouched original-file no-ops remain available even when an encoder cannot safely process that source.

Unknown meaningful precision is labelled unknown. When an integer decoder storage format is known, WAV can preserve that width conservatively; FLAC still requires proven meaningful integer precision.

Encoded FLAC has no predictable bitrate or size estimate. Maximum-size video processing with encoded FLAC is unavailable; use a bitrate-controlled audio codec or a video quality/bitrate target.

## Playback and waveform

Audio-only playback uses an audio player, with an AAC browser proxy when necessary. The audio timeline begins at the first selected sample and retains internal gaps as silence; video extraction retains its existing shared presentation clock.

One combined waveform shows channel extrema without downmixing playback or output. It is an overview with bounded resolution; cuts retain millisecond precision. Waveform decoding streams PCM, caches at most 200,000 peak pairs, and returns at most 4,096 pairs per visible window. It has a 120-second deadline and yields to processing after confirmed child termination.

Selection changes, removal, expiry, and workbench disconnection cancel obsolete analysis. Late responses cannot replace another selection or restore removed assets. Preview and waveform failures leave authored cuts and processing available.

## Compatibility and scope

Processing settings add `opus`, `flac`, and `wav` containers and `opus`, `flac`, and `pcm` audio choices. Inspection adds bounded sample-format and bit-depth facts; workspace descriptors add audio timeline and waveform status. `POST /api/workspace/waveform` starts/deduplicates preparation, GET returns a bounded time window, and DELETE cancels preparation. Existing fields and plan version remain compatible; no migration is required.

The v2.6.1 runtime checks, delayed-removal fixes, and serialized 0.5–1-second playlist pauses remain. Ordinary Download conversions and legacy conversion targets retain their contracts. Roadmap #7 remains incomplete; additional video codecs, hardware encoding, images, track controls, persistence, light mode, and packaging remain deferred.

This change is prepared for review. Merge, tagging, and release publication are separate steps.
